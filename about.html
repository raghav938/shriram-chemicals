About Shri Ram Chemicals

For over three decades, Shri Ram Chemicals has stood as a pioneer in the synthesis and compounding of high-performance wire drawing lubricants, pastes, and oils.

For over 30 years, Shri Ram Chemicals, based in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, India, has been a trusted name in the wire drawing industry. We are a leading manufacturer of calcium and sodium-based wire drawing powders, Drafil (paste), and wire drawing oil known for being cost-effective, efficient, and reliable.

Our powders are widely used across low carbon, high carbon, stainless wire de-scaled, galvanized (G.I.) wires, and copper wires—performing effectively from rod stage to fine wire drawing.

By combining high-grade raw materials with decades of expertise, Shri Ram Chemicals continues to set industry benchmarks in quality, innovation, and value-driven production.

Key Advantages of Our Products

Cost-Effective Solutions

Engineered to optimize wire mill production costs without compromising lubrication durability or die life.

High Efficiency

Delivers consistent drawing performance across all reduction drafts, high line speeds, and continuous passes.

Superior Wire Finish

Ensures smooth reduction and leaves clean, uniform residual films suitable for bright annealing, plating, or galvanizing.

Extended Die Life

Superior hydrodynamic and boundary layer lubrication that minimizes tungsten carbide and PCD die wear.

Custom Grades

Mesh particle distribution and carrier ratios custom-tailored for pickled, descaled, stainless, or copper wire drawing.
