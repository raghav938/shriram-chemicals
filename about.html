Trusted Name in the Wire Drawing Industry

For over 30 years, Shri Ram Chemicals, based in Ghaziabad, India, has been a trusted name in the wire drawing industry. We are a leading manufacturer of calcium and sodium-based wire drawing powders, specialized high-speed compounds, Drafil (paste), and wire drawing oil known for being cost-effective, efficient, and reliable.

Our powders are widely used across low carbon, high carbon, stainless wire de-scaled, galvanized (G.I.) wires and copper wires—performing effectively from rod stage to fine wire drawing.

By combining high-grade raw materials with decades of expertise, Shri Ram Chemicals continues to set industry benchmarks in quality, innovation, and value-driven production.
