About Shri Ram Chemicals

For over 30 years, Shri Ram Chemicals, based in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, India, has been a trusted name in the wire drawing industry. We are a leading manufacturer of calcium and sodium-based wire drawing powders, Drafil (paste), and specialized wire drawing oils known for being cost-effective, efficient, and reliable.

Our powders are widely used across low carbon, high carbon, stainless wire de-scaled, galvanized (G.I.) wires, and copper wires ? performing effectively from the heavy rod breakdown stage down to fine wire drawing passes.
