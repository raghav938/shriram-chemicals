Why Leading Copper Drawing Units Choose CU-56

1. Zero Annealing Stains

Unlike low-grade soluble oils sold on marketplace portals that leave sticky carbon soot on copper wire during annealing, CU-56 decomposes cleanly leaving no trace on the conductor.

2. Extended Die Longevity

High extreme-pressure (EP) boundary lubrication film shields PCD (polycrystalline diamond) and tungsten carbide dies, cutting tooling replacements by up to 35%.

3. Bio-Stable Anti-Foam System

Formulated with advanced bio-resistant agents that prevent bacterial sump souring, foul odor, and excessive foam generation under high pump pressure.
