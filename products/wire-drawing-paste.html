Process Engineering

Shop-Floor Operational Guidance

Practical machine-setup recommendations from over three decades of on-site wire mill technical troubleshooting.

precision_manufacturing

1. Direct Trough Application vs. Sump Emulsion

DRAFIL paste can be applied neat into open die boxes or pre-diluted with warm water (1:5 to 1:10 ratio) to recharge recirculating wet drawing sumps, offering immense flexibility across various machine architectures.

precision_manufacturing

2. Eliminating Wire Scratching & Galling

When drafting fine wire at high speeds, metal pickups inside the die cone cause scratch lines that ruin wire tensile uniformity. DRAFIL's high-tack boundary film cushions the reduction zone and continuously flushes fines.

precision_manufacturing

3. Capstan Grooving & Block Wear Prevention

High wire tension against machine drawing capstans can wear deep grooves into hard-chromed steel capstans. DRAFIL leaves a protective micro-film that drastically minimizes abrasive capstan wear.

precision_manufacturing

4. Flawless Downstream Surface Finishing

Because DRAFIL utilizes water-dispersible synthetic soaps, drawn wire emerges with a clean, light surface that dissolves rapidly in degreasing or rinsing baths, leaving zero carbon residue during furnace annealing.

Industrial Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline What is DRAFIL Wire Drawing Paste primarily used for?

DRAFIL Paste is designed for wet wire drawing machines, die immersion troughs, and wet bench capstans processing galvanized wire, high carbon fine wire, and low carbon steel wire where high surface finish and die protection are required.

help_outline How is DRAFIL Paste applied on the machine floor?

It can be applied as a neat paste directly in the die box or diluted with process water into a rich cooling emulsion for circulating sump systems. Plant technicians can adjust paste concentration based on draft severity.

help_outline Does DRAFIL Paste leave gummy deposits inside the die boxes or sump tanks?

No. DRAFIL is engineered with water-dispersible soaps that prevent polymer gumming or sludge formation, keeping pipelines, nozzles, and die troughs clean.
