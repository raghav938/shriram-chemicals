1. Stainless Steel Drawing Dynamics (Grade SS-1000)

Stainless steels (SS 304/316) work-harden aggressively in the very first reduction pass. SS-1000 provides high-temperature boundary film strength that cushions the tungsten carbide die, preventing metal pickup, micro-cracks, and wire snapping.

precision_manufacturing

2. Mechanical Descaling Protection (Grade 85 Black)

Mechanically descaled wire rods retain abrasive micro-islands of iron oxide. Standard drawing soaps break down under scale abrasion. 85 Black uses micronized graphite as a solid-state lubricant that envelops scale particles and shields the die bearing.

precision_manufacturing

3. CO2 MIG Welding Wire Drawing & Copper Adhesion

MIG welding wire requires precise surface residual thickness to permit uniform chemical or electrolytic copper coating. Our specialty drawing compound burns clean without leaving insulating residues that cause welding arc instability.

precision_manufacturing

4. Die Angle Optimization for Difficult Alloys

For stainless steel drawing with SS-1000, utilize tighter die reduction angles (8° to 10°) and ensure continuous water cooling across both die jacket and internal capstans to control intense frictional deformation heat.

Industrial Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline Why is SS-1000 required for drawing stainless steel instead of general MS wire drawing powder?

Stainless steel work-hardens rapidly and has poor natural heat conductivity, creating extreme contact friction inside the die. Standard MS powders melt too early and break down, causing severe die galling. SS-1000 delivers extreme-temperature film stability and prevents metal pickup on tungsten carbide or PCD dies.

help_outline What is the role of graphite in the 85 Black specialty drawing compound?

In mechanical descaling plants where acid pickling is bypassed, abrasive iron scale remains on the rod surface. 85 Black incorporates fine colloidal graphite that functions as a solid-state physical cushion, preventing abrasive wear on tungsten carbide dies.

help_outline Can these specialty compounds be washed off easily before bright annealing or plating?

Yes. SS-1000 is formulated with soluble sodium soaps that rinse cleanly in warm water or mild degreasers. MIG wire compounds leave minimal ash that does not impede chemical copper coating.
