Process Engineering

Shop-Floor Operational Guidance

Practical machine-setup recommendations from over three decades of on-site wire mill technical troubleshooting.

precision_manufacturing

1. Stainless Steel Work-Hardening Control

Austenitic stainless steels (SS 304/316) work-harden aggressively in the very first reduction draft. SS-1000 provides high-temperature sodium stearate film strength that cushions the die bearing, preventing metal pickup, micro-cracks, and wire snapping.

precision_manufacturing

2. Anti-Galling Boundary Lubrication

Stainless wire lacks natural lubricity and tends to cold-weld to tungsten carbide die profiles under friction. SS-1000 creates an impenetrable boundary barrier with high softening points that eliminates die galling and scratching.

precision_manufacturing

3. Complete Water & Degreaser Washability

Residual lubricant on stainless steel must be completely eliminated prior to bright annealing or electro-polishing. SS-1000 is 100% water-soluble and rinses away thoroughly in warm water baths without leaving carbon soot or burn stains.

precision_manufacturing

4. Die Angle Optimization for Stainless Alloys

For stainless wire drawing with SS-1000, utilize tighter die reduction half-angles (8° to 10°) and ensure continuous water cooling across both die jacket and internal capstans to moderate intense frictional deformation heat.

Industrial Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline Why is SS-1000 required for drawing stainless steel instead of general MS wire drawing powder?

Stainless steel work-hardens rapidly and has poor natural heat conductivity, creating extreme contact friction inside the die. Standard MS calcium powders melt too early and break down, causing severe die galling. SS-1000 delivers extreme-temperature film stability and prevents metal pickup on tungsten carbide or PCD dies.

help_outline What wire grades are compatible with SS-1000?

SS-1000 is engineered for austenitic, ferritic, and martensitic stainless steels including SS 304, SS 304L, SS 316, SS 316L, SS 302, SS 202, and high-nickel alloy wires.

help_outline Can SS-1000 residues be washed off easily before bright annealing?

Yes. SS-1000 is formulated with soluble sodium soaps that rinse cleanly in warm water or mild alkaline degreasers, leaving zero carbon residue that could cause discoloration during continuous atmospheric annealing.
