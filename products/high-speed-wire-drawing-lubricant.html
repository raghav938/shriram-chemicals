Operational Overview: High-Speed Wire Drawing Lubricant (Grade S-90)

1. What Is It: High-Speed Wire Drawing Lubricant (Grade S-90) is a specialized sodium-enriched dry lubricant formulated specifically for high-velocity continuous multi-block drawing machines.
2. Operational Mechanism: Engineered with anti-caking and rapid-fluidity micro-granules that dynamically collapse inward around the running wire rod in the die box, preventing cavity formation ('tunneling') at line speeds up to 20 m/s.
3. Thermal Stability: Features advanced thermal dissipation chemistry that rapidly draws friction heat away from the die contact zone, completely eliminating soap charring, die blistering, and tungsten carbide thermal shock.
4. Finish Quality: Yields an ultra-smooth, uniform microscopic lubricating film that preserves die geometry and prevents wire surface scuffing through 8 to 12 consecutive reduction stages.
5. Dispatch: Manufactured in Ghaziabad and supplied directly to steel wire drawing mills in 50 KG moisture-sealed HDPE bags.

1. Anti-Tunneling Dynamics at High Line Speed

At line speeds exceeding 12-15 m/s, standard coarse powders get thrown aside by wire vibration, creating a hollow tunnel where the wire enters the die dry. S-90's calibrated micro-granules maintain continuous dynamic roll into the wire entry cone.

precision_manufacturing

2. Friction Heat Dissipation Across Tandem Passes

Tandem passes without sufficient inter-die cooling generate cumulative heat that turns ordinary soap into gummy slag. S-90 provides a high-melting hydrodynamic barrier that sweeps through the die without glazing or adhering to the capstan.

precision_manufacturing

3. Die Box Seal & Pressure Box Optimization

For pressurized die boxes, S-90 maintains fluid compaction under back-pressure, creating an ultra-dense hydrodynamic lubricating wedge that lowers drafting amperage and reduces capstan motor load by 8% to 12%.

precision_manufacturing

4. Downstream Coating Compatibility

S-90 leaves a uniform, microscopically thin soap coat that does not interfere with inline copper coating on CO2 welding wire or downstream galvanizing flux baths, ensuring maximum bond strength and zero blister defects.

Industrial Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline How does S-90 prevent 'tunneling' in high-speed die boxes?

Tunneling happens when static powder forms a hollow cavity around high-speed traveling wire. S-90 utilizes balanced particle spherical morphology and anti-static surface treatments that ensure constant granular collapse toward the wire core, guaranteeing 100% lubricant uptake at speeds up to 20+ m/s.

help_outline Can S-90 be used on both low carbon and medium carbon wire drawing lines?

Yes. S-90 is specifically formulated for high-speed multi-pass lines processing low carbon mild steel (MS), binding wire, galvanized redrawing wire, and medium carbon steel grades.

help_outline Will switching to S-90 reduce our tungsten carbide die replacement frequency?

Yes. Because S-90 prevents dry-friction spikes and thermal shock inside the reduction cone, wire mills typically observe a 25% to 40% increase in total drawn tonnage per die set.
