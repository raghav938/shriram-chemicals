Operational Overview: Wet Wire Drawing Lubricant Concentrate

1. What Is It: Wet Wire Drawing Lubricants are water-soluble emulsion concentrates formulated for multi-die submerged spray and trough systems drawing intermediate to ultra-fine wire gauges.
2. Cooling Mechanism: Combines extreme pressure boundary film additives with rapid thermal convection, dissipating massive deformation heat generated during high-draft multi-die reductions.
3. Fines Management: Formulated with anti-clogging dispersants that prevent metal swarf, zinc dust, and iron fines from re-circulating into die orifices, preventing wire surface scoring and die wear.
4. Foam Resistance: Exhibits zero foaming even under high-pressure nozzle spraying and intense capstan turbulence in high-speed submerged baths.
5. Direct Supply: Manufactured by Shri Ram Chemicals in Ghaziabad, India and shipped in 200 KG industrial barrels with full technical mill support.

Process Engineering

Shop-Floor Operational Guidance

Practical machine-setup recommendations from over three decades of on-site wire mill technical troubleshooting.

precision_manufacturing

1. Sump Concentration & Emulsion Stability

Maintain bath concentration between 5% - 8% for galvanized wire and 8% - 12% for high-carbon fine wire. Use a handheld optical refractometer daily to monitor active solids and prevent under-lubrication.

precision_manufacturing

2. Zinc Fines & Swarf Separation Dynamics

In galvanized wire redrawing, zinc particulate strips off rapidly. Our wet lubricant features selective coagulation chemistry that drops zinc fines to the tank bottom rather than holding them in abrasive suspension.

precision_manufacturing

3. Foam Control in High-Pressure Spray Benches

Excessive foam starves submerged dies of actual lubricant, leading to sudden wire necking and breaks. Our formulation contains shear-stable defoamers that withstand continuous 3-5 bar pump recirculating nozzles.

precision_manufacturing

4. Die Lifespan & Diamond / Carbide Protection

Formulated with extreme-pressure organo-metallic esters that maintain a cushioning boundary barrier on natural diamond, PCD, and tungsten carbide dies, cutting tooling replacement costs substantially.

Industrial Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outline What is the recommended operating concentration for wet wire drawing machines?

For low-carbon mild steel and galvanized wire, an operating concentration of 4% to 7% is standard. For high carbon spring wire and high-tensile bead wire reductions, we recommend maintaining 8% to 12% concentration.

help_outline How does this wet lubricant prevent bacterial rotting in mill sumps?

Our formulation is engineered with advanced industrial biostabilizers and pH buffers that resist anaerobic bacterial breakdown, foul odors, and fungal growth even during prolonged plant weekend shutdowns.

help_outline Can this lubricant be used on galvanized wire without stripping the zinc coating?

Yes. The formulation is pH-balanced and chemically passivated to protect the zinc layer, preventing peeling and maintaining bright uniform metallic zinc coverage on finished wire.
