About Our Company

OVER 30 YEARS OF MANUFACTURING EXCELLENCE

For over 30 years, Shri Ram Chemicals, based in Ghaziabad, India, has been a trusted name in the wire drawing industry. We are a leading manufacturer of calcium and sodium-based wire drawing powders, specialized high-speed compounds, Drafil (paste), and wire drawing oil known for being cost-effective, efficient, and reliable.

Our powders are widely used across low carbon, high carbon, stainless wire de-scaled, galvanized (G.I.) wires and copper wires—performing effectively from rod breakdown stage to fine wire drawing.

By combining high-grade raw materials with decades of expertise, Shri Ram Chemicals continues to set industry benchmarks in quality, innovation, and value-driven production.

Low Carbon Wire Drawing Lubricants

GRADE D-45 (All Rounder)
Standard workhorse calcium powder for 5mm to 2mm wire drawing.

Enhanced stearate barrier minimizing carbide die wear on fast benches.
GRADE PKL (Pickled Wire Shine)
Specialty powder for acid-pickled rods ensuring mirror-like bright finish.

CATEGORY 03 • HIGH SPEED BENCHES 50 KG Bags

High Speed Wire Drawing Powders

Specially separated category for high-speed continuous multi-die wire drawing machines running at high velocities. Formulated for rapid die-box pickup, instant boundary film regeneration, and superior heat dissipation without thermal charring.

GRADE S-90 (Continuous High Speed 15-20 m/s)
Sodium-enriched compound designed for high-velocity multi-block drawing benches. Delivers consistent hydrodynamic film adherence, minimizes friction heat, and prevents die burn at high speeds.

CATEGORY 04 • WET BENCH COMPOUND 200 KG Drums

DRAFIL Wet Wire Drawing Paste

Water-dispersible synthetic drawing paste formulated for submerged wet drawing systems, fine galvanized wires, and mild steel continuous finishing. Provides extraordinary cooling, low foam formation, flushes fines from die cones, and prevents wire scratching.

GRADE DRAFIL PASTE (Synthetic Wet Drawing)
Supplied in 200 KG sealed industrial drums. Readily mixes with water into a stable lubricating emulsion for multi-cone submerged drawing machines.
