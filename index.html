About Shri Ram Chemicals

FOR OVER 30 YEARS: TRUSTED NAME IN THE WIRE DRAWING INDUSTRY

For over 30 years, Shri Ram Chemicals, based in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, India, has been a trusted name in the wire drawing industry. We are a leading manufacturer of calcium and sodium-based wire drawing powders, Drafil (paste), and wire drawing oil known for being cost-effective, efficient, and reliable.

Our powders are widely used across low carbon, high carbon, stainless wire de-scaled, galvanized (G.I.) wires, and copper wires—performing effectively from rod stage to fine wire drawing.

By combining high-grade raw materials with decades of expertise, Shri Ram Chemicals continues to set industry benchmarks in quality, innovation, and value-driven production under the commercial leadership of Mr. Kamal Mahajan.

CATEGORY 01 • 5MM TO 2MM 50 KG Bags

Low Carbon Wire Drawing Lubricants

Engineered for mild steel (MS), construction binding wire, and electrode wire. Features high lime carrier content to endure severe frictional heat during breakdown and continuous passes.

D-45 (All Rounder)
Standard workhorse calcium powder for 5mm to 2mm wire drawing.
P-75 (Better Die Life)
Enhanced stearate barrier minimizing carbide die wear on fast benches.
PKL (Pickled Wire Shine)
Specialty powder for acid-pickled rods ensuring mirror-like bright finish.
85 Black (Heavy Draft)
Extreme pressure graphite soap for heavy reductions & descaled wire.
