MANUFACTURING HERITAGE

For Over 30 Years: Trusted Name in
Wire Drawing Industry

A three-decade legacy of formulation excellence, chemical synthesis, and value-driven lubrication in Sahibabad Industrial Area, Ghaziabad.

For over 30 years, Shri Ram Chemicals, based in Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, India, has been a trusted name in the wire drawing industry. We are a leading manufacturer of calcium and sodium-based wire drawing powders, Drafil (paste), and wire drawing oil known for being cost-effective, efficient, and reliable.

Our powders are widely used across low carbon, high carbon, stainless wire de-scaled, galvanized (G.I.) wires, and copper wires—performing effectively from the heavy rod breakdown stage down to fine wire drawing passes.

By combining high-grade raw materials with decades of chemical compounding expertise, Shri Ram Chemicals continues to set industry benchmarks in quality, innovation, and value-driven production.
