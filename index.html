ENGINEERING CONSULTATION

Technical Selection: Calcium vs Sodium vs Oil

DRY CALCIUM SOAPS

When to Select Calcium

Calcium stearate formulations act as hard boundary lubricants with high melting points (140°C–160°C). Ideal for:

  • ✔ Mechanically descaled or pickled wire rods
  • ✔ High-pressure breakdown dies (first passes)
  • ✔ Low and medium carbon steel, MS & binding wire
  • ✔ Operations requiring thick residual protective films
WATER-SOLUBLE SODIUM SOAPS

When to Select Sodium

Sodium stearate provides superior lubricity at higher drawing velocities (12–25 m/s) with exceptional cleanliness:

  • ✔ High-speed multi-pass continuous drawing benches
  • ✔ Galvanized (GI) wire & bright wire applications
  • ✔ Wires requiring post-draw electroplating or coating
  • ✔ Completely water-washable and leaves zero carbon soot in annealing
CU-56 OIL / WET EMULSIONS

When to Select CU-56 Copper Oil

CU-56 is engineered exclusively for high-heat non-ferrous copper wire rod drawing operations:

  • ✔ Copper rod breakdown from 8.0mm to 1.2mm
  • ✔ Submerged / spray multi-die copper drawing machines
  • ✔ Prevents copper fines agglomeration and oxidation
  • ✔ Superior bright anneal compatibility with zero copper staining

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Procurement & Technical FAQs

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wire drawing powder?

Our standard bulk commercial MOQ starts at 500 KG to 1 Metric Ton (supplied in 50 KG HDPE bags). However, for new wire drawing mills conducting machine qualification trials, we provide 1–2 KG trial sample kits.

How does CU-56 copper wire drawing oil prevent staining?

CU-56 contains specialized oxidation inhibitors and copper passivators that prevent the formation of dark copper oxides even under high interfacial temperatures. It burns completely clean in inline bright annealing tubes.

Can you formulate custom mesh sizes or lime-to-soap ratios?

Yes. As a primary manufacturer, we can adjust particle size (mesh distribution) and soap-to-carrier ratios depending on whether your plant runs mechanical descaling, acid pickling, or borax coating.

What is your standard delivery lead time for North India?

Because our manufacturing unit is centrally located in Sahibabad (Ghaziabad), shipments to Delhi-NCR, Meerut, Faridabad, Sonipat, and western UP are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Interstate consignments (Punjab, Rajasthan, Gujarat) take 2 to 4 days.
